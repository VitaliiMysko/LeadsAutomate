Leads Automate's single purpose is to speed up lead research in LinkedIn Sales Navigator search: in a side panel next to a Sales Navigator people search, the user ranks the job titles they are looking for and gives a list of companies; the extension searches those companies one by one at a human pace, picks the matching people by the user's job-title priorities and hands each one to the companion Lead Generator extension to save. On a Sales Navigator company search, it exports the companies found to a CSV file.
