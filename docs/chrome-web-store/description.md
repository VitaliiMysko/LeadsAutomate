Leads Automate is a side panel for LinkedIn Sales Navigator that turns a list of target companies into saved leads, without searching each company and scrolling through its people by hand. It works together with the Lead Generator extension, which extracts and saves each lead.

Open a Sales Navigator people search, click the Leads Automate icon, and the panel opens next to the page. It is available only on Sales Navigator.

**Prioritize job titles**

- The panel lists the job titles you selected in the search's "Current job title" filter.
- Drag them into columns (for example Management and Technical), order them by priority, and set how many leads each title and each column should give.
- Join titles that mean the same thing (synonyms, other languages) into a group, or type titles of your own.
- Save the board to a file and load it back later.

**Search company by company**

- Load a list of companies: a text file of names, or a CSV with LinkedIn company ids, LinkedIn page links or names (optionally with logo images, used to confirm the right company).
- For each company, the extension sets Sales Navigator's "Current company" filter, reads the results and picks the people whose job titles match your priorities.
- Each picked lead is opened and passed to Lead Generator, which extracts the details, generates an email and saves the lead.
- Search one company at a time, or the whole list in one run. When a company can't be identified with certainty, it goes to a list for you to choose yourself.

**Stay in control**

- Every action on the page is paced like a person: randomized pauses between steps, pages and companies.
- Optional limits: leads per company, a daily limit of new leads, and failed leads in a row per company.
- The run stops by itself when LinkedIn reports its request limit, and you can stop it at any time.
- Statistics show pages read, leads processed and found, and the progress of the current company. A log shows every step.

**Export companies**

On a Sales Navigator company search, export the companies found (LinkedIn id and name) to a CSV file, which loads back as a company list. An interrupted export can be continued later.

**Privacy**

The extension runs only on LinkedIn Sales Navigator and stores everything locally in your browser: your job-title board, settings, company list, statistics and log. It has no server of its own and sends no data anywhere. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Requires the Lead Generator extension and a LinkedIn Sales Navigator account.
