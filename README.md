# Leads Automate

Chrome side-panel extension (Manifest V3) for **LinkedIn Sales Navigator people search** (`linkedin.com/sales/search/people`). It prioritizes the job titles from the search's "Current job title" filter and runs company-by-company searches through the "Current company" filter. For each company it takes leads by job-title priority and saves them through the [Lead generator](../LeadGenerator) extension.

## Features (v0.6)

The panel shows these sections, top to bottom:
1. status area;
2. Company;
3. Companies file;
4. **Statistics**: the results pages read, the leads processed, found, and the success rate (see *Statistics* below);
5. **Settings**: the settings of every search (*Result pages to check*, *Daily limit of found leads*);
6. **Job titles**: a collapsible section holding the Job list and the job title columns (Management and Technical to start with). Click its header to hide or show it. The header always shows the counts (titles in the filter, titles in each column), and the open / closed state is remembered;
7. Log.

Every section except the status area can be collapsed to its header: click the header (or its title) to hide or show the rest; the header's own buttons (Reset, Copy, Clear) still work while it's collapsed. The open / closed state of each section is remembered.

- **Job list**: every job title selected in Sales Navigator's *Current job title* filter (included values only). It refreshes whenever the search URL changes.
- **Columns**: the board starts with a *Management* and a *Technical* column; you can have any number of them, each with its own name and **Leads** value.
  - **+ Add column** (under the columns) adds an empty column at the end, named *Column N*, with its name selected so you can type one.
  - **Name**: click a column's name to change it (Enter or leaving the field saves it; an empty name keeps the previous one).
  - **← / →** in a column's header move it left or right. The columns' order is their priority: lead selection tries the leftmost column first. Two columns fit side by side; more wrap to the next rows, and their colors follow their position.
  - **×** removes a column. A column holding titles asks for a second click (*Remove?*); its titles go back to the Job list, and its manual titles are dropped. The last column can't be removed.
- **Column titles**: drag titles from the Job list into a column, reorder them within it, or move them to another column. The first title in a column has the highest priority. The columns grow with their titles (the *Job titles* section can be collapsed to save room); a newly added title is scrolled into view. Hover a title to see ↑ / ↓ / × buttons (× sends it back to the Job list). A title that is no longer in the filter stays in its column, shown struck through.
  - **Manual entry**: type a title into the *Add job title* field under a column and press Enter or **+**. It goes to the end of that column, dashed and marked *manual*. If the title is already on the board, in the Job list, or differs only in case or spacing, the existing spelling is used and moved to this column (out of its group, if it was in one). × on a manual title removes it.
  - **Groups**: titles close in meaning (synonyms, the same title in other languages) can be joined into a group, which takes one place in the column's priority order. Hover a title or group and click **⤴** to join it with the item above: a single title above becomes a group with both, a group above takes in the titles. Drag a title onto a group's titles to add it there, or out of the group to take it out; **×** on a title in a group takes it out of the group and puts it right after it. The group's name is optional (click it to type one; *Group of N* otherwise). **✂** splits the group back into single titles. Drag a group by its header to reorder it or move it to another column; dropped on the Job list, its titles go back there.
  - **Lead count per item**: every item of a column, a title or a group, has its own number (0–9, 1 by default): how many leads to take with that title, or with any of the group's titles together. The counts of a column may add up to more than the column's **Leads** value; that is expected, since the items are tried in priority order. A title keeps its count when it's moved; a title taken out of a group or split from it starts at 1, and a new group keeps the count of the item above.
- **Save to file** / **Load from file** (under the columns): saves all the columns as a JSON file (`job-titles-<date>.json`, format version 2), with their names, order and **Leads** values, the *Leads per company* setting, the groups and their names, every item's lead count and the *manual* marks, and loads such a file back. Loading replaces all the columns; save the current ones first if you want to keep them. Files saved by earlier versions (fixed Management / Technical columns) load as those two columns. A file that isn't a saved job titles file is rejected with an error in the log, and the board stays as it was. Titles of the loaded file that aren't in the current filter are shown struck through, as usual.
- **Leads** fields: the number of leads each column wants per search (0–9), used by *Lead selection*.
- **Leads per company** (above the columns; off by default, 1–99, 5 by default): turned on by its checkbox, it caps the leads found (saved by Lead generator, *Already saved* included) for one company in all the columns together. Once the company has that many, lead selection stops, even if some columns still want leads, no further results page is read, and the work goes on with the next company (**Search all**, **Search next**). The value is saved with the board and in **Save to file**; the section header shows it while it's on (*max 5 per company*).
- **Lead selection**: after every company search that shows leads (**Search**, **Search next**, **Search all**), the extension reads the leads of the results page (name, job title, company, location, time in role / company), scrolling down the list so every row renders. It then picks leads by the columns (`src/utils/lead-picker.js`):
  - the columns from left to right; each column's items from the highest priority down;
  - a lead's job title is split into the titles it holds, at `and`, `at` (whole words, any case), `&`, `/`, `|`, `,`, `(`, `)`, `[`, `]`, `@` and `;`: *Technical Director at Acme (CTO)* → *Technical Director*, *Acme*, *CTO*;
  - an item takes the leads with one of its titles as one of those titles, or as the whole job title, in full and in any letter case (punctuation and spaces aside: *Co-founder* = *Co Founder*). *Director* matches *Director, Founder* but not *Director Of Operations*; *Founder* doesn't match *Co-Founder and Chairman*. Leads are taken in page order, up to the item's count;
  - only a lead saved by Lead generator (below), now or by an earlier search, counts toward the item's count and the column's **Leads** value; a lead that isn't saved makes way for the next matching one. A column stops once it has its **Leads** value, and the whole company once it has its *Leads per company* (when on); a lead is tried once (also across columns);
  - a locked lead (out of your network, shown with a *View profile* button: its page holds no details) is never taken, nor one without a link to its page. Locked leads are found by the button's attributes (`leadLocked` in `src/content/selectors.js`), not its text; the log lists the skipped ones.

  Leads are tried one at a time in that order. A lead is opened by clicking its name, after a 3 s pause (`beforeLeadClickMs` in `src/constants/config.js`, a placeholder for now), which shows its details (the content of its `linkedin.com/sales/lead/` page) over the results. The opening is checked by the tab URL turning into the lead's URL, or its name heading showing on the page; a click the page doesn't handle is cancelled, so the tab never leaves the search. Then, each after a random 1.5–3 s pause, the [Lead generator](../LeadGenerator) extension:
  1. **Extract**s the lead and waits for its company's details (website, members…);
  2. **Generate**s the email; without an email the lead isn't saved;
  3. **Save**s the lead. A lead Lead generator had already saved (*Already saved*) counts as found too.

  At the end the tab is put back on the search URL. The log lists the leads read, the plan of each column (`DEBUG`), every lead tried with what Lead generator extracted, the email and the outcome, and how many leads each column saved against its **Leads** value.
- **Statistics** (its own card): *Pages* is how many search results pages were read for leads, *Processed* how many leads were tried (opened and given to Lead generator), *Found* how many of them were saved with an email (also *Already saved*), *Success* the share of found leads among the processed ones (— before any lead), and *Daily limit* the new leads found today against the limit (see *Daily limit of found leads*). The first four add up over all searches (**Search**, **Search next**, **Search all**, the list's **Search**), are updated after every lead, remembered after the panel is closed, and set back to zero only by **Reset**.
  - **Company leads** (the last row of the card): the progress of the company being processed toward the leads it was asked for, *found / wanted*, where *wanted* is the columns' **Leads** values together, at most *Leads per company* when that is on. The bar turns green once the company has them all. Under it, a chip per column (in the column's color) shows its own *found / wanted*, outlined once the column is full. It updates after every lead tried, keeps the last company's result once it's done, until the next lead selection, and is cleared by **Reset**.
- **Result pages to check** (Settings card, 1–100, 1 by default): Sales Navigator shows the results 25 leads per page, and lead selection reads up to this many pages, starting from the page shown. It goes on to the next page only while the company hasn't reached *Leads per company* (when on), some column still needs leads and has an item that can still take one, and only when the page was full (25 leads; a shorter page is the last one). Before each page there's a random 2.5–5 s pause (`betweenPagesMs` in `src/constants/config.js`). The page is changed the way Back / Forward does, through the URL's `page` parameter. If the results don't change within 15 s, its pagination button is clicked instead (`paginationPage` / `paginationNext` in `src/content/selectors.js`). Each move is checked by the URL showing that page and a different first lead; when neither way works, the log says so and selection stops at the pages read. Leads are numbered across the pages (#26 is the first lead of page 2), a lead is tried once, and at the end the tab goes back to the page it started on. The value is remembered.
- **Daily limit of found leads** (Settings card, off by default; 1–1000, 50 by default): turned on by its checkbox, it stops the work once that many new leads were found (saved by Lead generator) today; a lead *Already saved* by an earlier search isn't counted. The count of the day is shown in Statistics as *Daily limit*: a progress bar with *12 / 50* (only the count, *12 / unlimited*, without a bar while the setting is off; the bar and the outline turn orange once the limit is reached), starts over at local midnight, and isn't affected by **Reset** in Statistics. The company being processed is always finished, even when the limit is reached during it (so the day's count may end above the limit); after that no new company search starts (**Search**, **Search next**, **Search all**, the list's **Search**). The status area and the log give the reason, and **Search all** stops, leaving the next company to search. Raise the limit or turn it off to go on.
- **Lead generator**: must be installed and enabled; it's found among the installed extensions by its name. When its panel isn't open on the search tab, the extension opens it there itself after a random 0.8–2 s pause, as a click on its icon does, and waits up to 15 s for it to load (the log says *Opened the Lead generator panel*). It answers only this extension, whose id the manifest `key` fixes (`ofbkinmakmfacepccjcbmjgecdppolki`). When it isn't installed, its panel can't be opened (a version without that, or the page doesn't show its icon) or doesn't answer, or its saved leads limit is reached, the search stops with the reason in the status area and the log, and **Search all** stops too.
- **No matches**: when Sales Navigator shows *No matches found* (a filter, e.g. *Current job title*, leaves nobody), the search counts as having no leads and no lead is opened. The banner is recognized by its illustration (`empty-room`), not its text.
- **LinkedIn's request limit**: once LinkedIn refuses a Sales Navigator request with HTTP 429 (*Too many requests*: the account's daily limit is used up), the search stops at its next page step, with the reason and the time in the status area and the log, and **Search all** stops too, leaving the company to search again. Each new search forgets an earlier refusal, so it tries again and stops at once while the limit holds.
- **Required job titles**: every search (**Search**, **Search next**, **Search all**, and **Search** in the *Needs manual choice* list) needs at least one title or group in some column. With every column empty the search doesn't start: the status area and the log show an error, and the *Job titles* section opens.
- **Company → Search**: removes every value already in the *Current company* filter (included and excluded), types the name and picks the company from the dropdown, then waits for the results.
  - Old values are removed without reloading the page. The extension switches the page to the same search URL without the company filter, the way Back / Forward does, and Sales Navigator updates the filters (about 0.2 s). Script-generated clicks on the remove buttons have no effect. If the page doesn't follow, the search URL is reloaded without the filter, and a `DEBUG` snapshot of the selected value goes to the log.
  - The dropdown option is selected with the keyboard, because Sales Navigator ignores script-generated clicks and hovering. The arrow keys start from the option Sales Navigator highlights when the list opens, so the extension presses ↑ or ↓ toward the chosen option, then Enter. If the chosen option is already highlighted, it steps one option away and back, because Enter on that initial highlight adds the typed text as free text instead of the company.
  - The applied value in the URL must be the chosen option and carry a LinkedIn company id. A different company or free text is logged as an error, removed, and the search counts as failed. A wrong company never stays applied.
  - **Automatic choice**. Options "with the same name" match the typed name ignoring case, legal form, punctuation and spaces, e.g. *Acme* ↔ *ACME GmbH*, *Factory 73 Ltd* ↔ *Factory73*. The rules are tried in this order:
    1. nothing is chosen when the dropdown is empty or no option resembles the name (no word starting with the name's first word); that's what LinkedIn shows when it finds nothing;
    2. the first option, when it has the same name. If it differs from the typed name only in case and a lower option matches exactly (case-sensitive), that lower option;
    3. nothing is chosen when the first option with the same name isn't the first in the list: in labelled choices such options were right only about half the time;
    4. the dropdown's only option;
    5. otherwise, by logo (see *Logo check*).

    When no option has the same name and none is chosen by logo, the extension types a shorter name, up to 2 times (`SHORTER_NAME_ATTEMPTS` in `src/constants/config.js`), after a random 1.5–3.5 s pause each time. The steps add up in this order, and each one that changes the name gives the next name to try (`src/utils/name-variants.js`):
    1. without the legal form: *LAIT ApS* → *LAIT*;
    2. without a country or region: *Knowit Denmark* → *Knowit*, *True Digital (US)* → *True Digital*;
    3. without a tagline after ` - `, ` | `, `, `, ` (` or `: `: *Codiance - Coded Brilliance* → *Codiance*;
    4. without a trailing *Group*: *IDHL Group* → *IDHL*.

    For example, *itm8 Sverige AB* is tried as *itm8 Sverige*, then *itm8*. The same rules choose from the new dropdown, except that its only option isn't taken unless it has the same name. When nothing is chosen, the last dropdown stays open for a manual choice.

    LinkedIn's own order of the options is the strongest signal: in 251 labelled choices, the first option with the same name was right in about 95% of cases. The log names the rule that chose the company, or the reason no company could be chosen.
  - **Manual choice**: when no rule applies, **Search**, **Search next** and the list's **Search** leave the dropdown open and wait up to 5 min for you to pick the company in Sales Navigator. **Stop** cancels the wait. A cancelled or timed-out wait puts the company on the *Needs manual choice* list.
- **Companies file**: one of two formats. Duplicates and empty entries are skipped. **File format** under **Choose file** expands a short description of the formats and CSV columns, with an example. When a `.csv` file is chosen, a dialog lists the columns found before the list is loaded (`Columns found: company_id, linkedin, company_name, logo.`), with a warning when there is no `company_id`, `linkedin` or `company_name` column (the file is then read as a list of names). The list is loaded once you click **OK**. The log gives the details: which file column is read as each one, the columns not found and the ignored ones.
  - A text file with company names separated by commas (line breaks also work).
  - A CSV with a header row and at least one of three columns that tell which company a row is. Each row uses the strongest one it has: `company_id` (LinkedIn company id, exact), then `linkedin` (the company's LinkedIn page URL, from which the id is read, see *LinkedIn page* below), then `company_name` (typed in the filter, the company chosen among the suggestions). An optional `logo` column holds the path of the company's logo image on this computer. Columns may be in any order, separated by `,` or `;`. A row without a name is named by its id, else by the name part of its page URL (`thisistangent` for `/company/thisistangent/`) until the page is read: it then takes the company's name on LinkedIn (`Tangent`), with its logo and manual-choice list entry, and the log says so. That name is the label of the filter value, and is typed if the page can't be read. Rows with the same id, else the same LinkedIn page, else the same name, are kept once.
  - **Company id**: the number (`2781504`), a URN (`urn:li:organization:2781504`) or a company page URL with the number (`https://www.linkedin.com/company/2781504/`, `.../sales/company/2781504`). A page URL by name (`/company/acme`) doesn't carry the number. Such ids are listed in the log when the file is loaded, and those companies are searched by name.
  - **LinkedIn page**: when `company_id` is empty, the id is taken from the `linkedin` column. A URL with the number is used as is. For a URL by name (`https://www.linkedin.com/company/acme/`, also `/showcase/` and `/school/`), the id is read from the company's public LinkedIn page when the company is searched, after a random 0.8–2 s pause. A company or showcase page is loaded without your LinkedIn cookies, as a logged-out visitor, so the request doesn't come from your account. LinkedIn shows school pages only to members (a logged-out visitor gets HTTP 999), so a `/school/` page is loaded with your LinkedIn session, as if you opened it once; the log marks these "with your LinkedIn session", and LinkedIn must be signed in in this browser. Only the page's own company id is used (its top card), not those of the similar companies it lists. Ids found, with the company's name on LinkedIn, are remembered by page, so each page is read once. If the page can't be read (missing page, LinkedIn's request limit or sign-in wall), the log says why and the company is searched by name; the page is tried again on its next search. Values that aren't LinkedIn page URLs are listed in the log when the file is loaded.
  - **Search by id**: a company with an id skips the typeahead, the rules and the logos. After a random 0.8–2 s pause the extension switches the page to the same search URL with *Current company* set to just that id, labelled with the file's name, the way Back / Forward does. It counts as done once the URL holds only that id and the page shows one selected company that it didn't show before. The page shows LinkedIn's name for it (*American Vacuum Society (AVS)* for the file's *AVS*), so that name isn't compared with the file's. If the page doesn't follow, the URL is loaded instead; that reload closes the Lead generator panel, which lead selection then opens again. If neither works, the company is searched by name as usual. No match record is kept for these searches. The log warns when the search shows no leads, because the id may be wrong. In manual labeling the name search always runs, and the log says whether your pick has the file's id; an automatic or manual pick made after a failed search by id is checked the same way.
  - **Logos**: everything about logos (the **Choose logos folder** button with the logos count, the logos strip while a company is processed, the logo check) shows and works only when the loaded CSV has a `logo` column; with an empty column the button shows *the logo column is empty*. Click **Choose logos folder** and pick the folder that holds them. The files are matched to the companies by file name. PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP and SVG are read. An SVG is drawn at full hash size whatever its declared size. A file that can't be read is listed in the log with the reason. The extension keeps each logo's fingerprint (a perceptual hash) and a thumbnail, so you only need to pick the folder again after loading a new CSV.
  - **Search next**: searches the next company in the list.
  - **Search all**: searches the remaining companies one by one, with a random pause of 6–12 s between them. It never waits for you: a company that can't be chosen automatically goes to the *Needs manual choice* list and the run continues. At the end, the status area and the log report how many companies need a manual choice, and which ones. **Stop** ends the run after the current company. **↺** restarts the list from the beginning.
  - **‹** / **›**: move the list's position back or forward by one company without searching, while no search runs. **‹** makes the previous company the next one to search (to search it again), **›** skips the current one. The *Name company* field shows the company at the new position, and the progress bar follows.
  - The progress bar shows processed / total companies. The company being processed is shown in the *Name company* field.
  - The list and the current position are remembered after the panel is closed.
  - **Needs manual choice**: companies waiting for a manual choice, each with the reason. **Search** runs that company with a manual choice, and the company leaves the list once its filter is applied. **×** or **Clear** removes companies from the list.
- **Logo check**: when the company being searched has a logo from the CSV and the dropdown options show logos, the extension compares them. It uses a perceptual hash that copes with differences in size, padding, JPEG compression, background, and light-on-dark versions. The log lists each option's similarity, and the Companies file card shows both logos with their similarity while the company is processed.
  - A matching logo confirms the choice made by name. A different logo never rejects or changes it: the file's logo differed from the LinkedIn one for about two thirds of the right companies, and options with the same name often share a logo.
  - When the name rules can't choose, the one option whose logo clearly matches is chosen (closest within the match distance, clearly ahead of the next), as long as its name resembles the typed one.
  - The thresholds are in `LOGO_THRESHOLDS` (`src/constants/config.js`). They were set on the 170 Umbraco partner logos compared with small JPEG copies: the same logo is within 10 in about 75% of cases, and different logos are 20 or more apart in about 99.8% of cases.
- **Manual labeling** (toggle in the Companies file card): for building a labelled set. Every search types the name and waits for your pick in the dropdown; nothing is selected automatically. What the rules would have chosen is only predicted. The log shows the prediction and whether you agreed with it, and the record keeps both. **Search all** is unavailable in this mode, so go through the file with **Search next**. Choose the logos folder first, so the records include the logo comparisons. **Clean**, shown only in this mode, deletes all match records (auto and manual) so a labelling session starts from an empty set. Click it twice within 4 s to confirm, and **Export** the records first if you want to keep them.
- **Match records** (internal, never written to disk on their own): every company choice is stored inside the extension. Automatic choices go under `auto`; picks made by hand go under `manual`, as `mode` `fallback` (the rules couldn't choose) or `labeling`. **Export** downloads all records as a JSON file; the card shows how many there are. A record holds:
  - `name` (from the companies file), `typed` (the name whose dropdown the record describes: a shorter one if the search retyped it), `attempts` (the names typed before it, each with its option count and why nothing was chosen);
  - `chosen`, `companyId` (the chosen company's LinkedIn id, e.g. `2781504` from `urn:li:organization:2781504`), `chosenIndex` / `chosenInList` (position of the chosen company in the dropdown, `-1` if you picked something else);
  - `prediction`: what the rules chose or would have chosen (`index`, `label`, `rule` or `reason`, the name rule's own pick `nameIndex` / `nameRule`, and the logo verdict). `predictionCorrect` says whether it matched your pick (manual records only);
  - `options`, one entry per dropdown option:
    - `label` and full `text` (detail lines such as the industry);
    - `logo`: `logo` / `ghost` / `none`;
    - `logoDistance` / `logoSimilarity` against the CSV logo;
    - `highlighted`: whether the list opened with this option highlighted;
    - `name` features: exact, ignoring case, ignoring the legal form, starts with, contains, word overlap, edit similarity;
  - `csv` (`companyId`, `linkedin`, `hasLogo` from the companies file; `companyId` there is the right answer for checking `companyId` of the choice), `leads` (leads on the page after the choice) and `pickMs` (how long a manual pick took).

  Only the last 5000 records of each kind are kept. The records carry a format version: when the panel starts and finds records of an older format, it removes them and logs how many it removed.
- **Status area**: shows the current operation, how long it has been running, and its outcome or total time.
- **Log**: a scrollable list with time-stamped `DEBUG` / `INFO` / `OK` / `WARN` / `ERROR` entries, with Copy (copies the visible entries) and Clear buttons. Lines marked `[page]` come from the script running on the LinkedIn page.
  - **Quick filters**: one toggle per marker with its entry count. Click a toggle to show or hide that marker, double-click it to show only that marker, or click **All** to show everything. The selection is remembered.

Board (including manual titles), lead counts, company name, the companies list, the logos' hashes and thumbnails, the manual choice list, match records (key `companyMatchRecords`) and the log filter selection are kept in `chrome.storage.local`.

## Install (development)

1. `chrome://extensions/` → enable **Developer mode** → **Load unpacked** → select this folder.
2. Reload any Sales Navigator tabs that were already open.
3. On a Sales Navigator tab, click the extension's toolbar icon to open the side panel. The panel is available only on `linkedin.com/sales/*` tabs.
4. Install a [Lead generator](../LeadGenerator) version with the companion API (after 3.5.0), which lets this extension open its panel. With an older one, open its panel on the search tab yourself.

The manifest `key` gives the extension the same id wherever it's loaded from. An install from before the key (v0.5) had a different id, so Chrome treats this as a new extension and its stored data (board, companies list, logos, match records) doesn't carry over: **Save to file** the job titles and **Export** the match records before reloading.

The `management` permission is used only to find Lead generator among the installed extensions. The `webRequest` permission (with `linkedin.com/sales-api/*`) only reads the status codes of Sales Navigator's requests, to notice LinkedIn's request limit (HTTP 429).

Requires Chrome 116+.

## How it works

- `src/background.js` enables the side panel only on Sales Navigator tabs.
- The side panel (`sidepanel.html`, `src/sidepanel/`) does all the orchestration. It reads the search filters from the tab URL: Sales Navigator stores them in the `query` parameter in Rest.li syntax, parsed by `src/utils/restli-query.js`. This works in any interface language and doesn't depend on LinkedIn's hashed class names. The URL is also used to check that each step (removing the old company, applying the new one) really happened.
- The content script (`src/content/`) performs the individual page steps:
  - switch the page to a URL without a filter (in-page navigation);
  - type into the filter and return the dropdown's option names;
  - select a given option with the keyboard;
  - close the dropdown;
  - wait for results;
  - read the leads of the results page and open one by its name.

  The side panel decides which option to select (`src/utils/company-match.js`). All DOM selectors are in `src/content/selectors.js`; when Sales Navigator's markup changes, update them there.

## Notes

LinkedIn's User Agreement restricts automated activity. The extension paces its actions (randomized delays, character-by-character typing, pauses between companies), but runs should still be moderate in volume.
