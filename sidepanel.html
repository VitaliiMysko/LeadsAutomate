File format

A .txt file: company names separated by commas or line breaks.

A .csv file: a header row, then one company per row. Separated by , or ;; columns in any order, any letter case; other columns are ignored. It needs at least one of company_id, linkedin, company_name. Each row uses the strongest one it has, in this order:

company_id
LinkedIn company id: 2781504, urn:li:organization:2781504 or a page URL with the number. Selects the company exactly, without the typeahead.
linkedin
Company page URL. Its id is read from the page when the company is searched (a /school/ page with your LinkedIn session, others as a logged-out visitor), then used as company_id. A row without company_name takes the company's name on LinkedIn.
company_name
Name typed in the Current company filter, the company chosen among the suggestions. Also the name shown for the row.
logo
Path of the logo image on this computer; then Choose logos folder. Helps choose between suggestions with similar names.

Without an id, a company is searched by name.
